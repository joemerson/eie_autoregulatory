# Patent Notice

## What is covered by patent

The following Brazilian patent application covers the **method and system** described
in this repository:

> **BR 10 2025 024406-3**
> Filed: November 8, 2025
> Inventor: Joemerson da Silva Lima — Electrical Engineer
> Cuiabá, Mato Grosso, Brazil

The patent covers:

- The method of computing a composite informational-energetic efficiency metric
  combining statistical loss, energetic proxy cost, and informational cost (L2 norm
  or compressibility of the latent vector)
- The self-regulatory control system that conditionally stops or adjusts training
  based on the EIE metric after a data regime change
- The integrated audit module generating CSV logs, JSON metadata and PDF reports
- The non-transitory storage medium containing instructions to execute the method
